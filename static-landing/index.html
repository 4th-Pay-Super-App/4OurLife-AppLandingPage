About 4 Our Life

Our Mission

4 Our Life is a Ghanaian health technology company on a mission to make quality health information and wellness tools accessible to every Ghanaian, regardless of location or income. We believe that proactive health management should be simple, trustworthy, and built for local realities.

The Story

Founded in 2025, 4 Our Life began when our founders saw how difficult it was for ordinary Ghanaians to find reliable health guidance, track medications, or locate verified wellness facilities. What started as a symptom-checker prototype has grown into an all-in-one platform serving fitness plans, disease information, medication reminders, group health communities, and a curated directory of verified wellness centers across Ghana.

What We Do

  • Health Education: Doctor-reviewed articles on diseases, symptoms, and healthy living tailored to Ghanaian contexts.
  • Digital Tools: Fitness trackers, medication reminders, and symptom checkers that work offline.
  • Community: Moderated group chats where users support each other on topics like diabetes, maternal health, and fitness.
  • Facility Verification: We partner with HEFRA to verify clinics, pharmacies, and wellness centers so users know where to go.

Our Values

  • Trust First: Every piece of health content is reviewed or sourced from accredited institutions.
  • Privacy by Design: Your health data is encrypted and never sold to third parties.
  • Local Relevance: We prioritize malaria, hypertension, diabetes, and maternal health because these matter most in Ghana.
  • Inclusion: Our app works on low-end devices and poor network connections.

Team & Culture

We are a remote-first team of health technologists, medical advisors, designers, and community managers spread across Accra, Kumasi, and Tamale. We move fast, think locally, and always put the user first.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 24, 2026

4 Our Life Health Tech (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our website, mobile application, and services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically, and information from third-party sources.

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender, and password when you register.
  • Health Information: Symptoms checked, fitness goals, workout history, medication schedules, weight, height, and any health data you voluntarily enter.
  • Device Information: IP address, device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, click patterns, and app crash logs.
  • Location Data: With your consent, we collect precise or approximate location to show nearby wellness centers and fitness facilities.
  • Communications: Messages sent in group chats, support tickets, and feedback submissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our Services.
  • To personalize your experience, including fitness recommendations and health tips.
  • To send medication reminders, appointment notifications, and wellness updates.
  • To verify and display wellness centers and healthcare facilities near you.
  • To moderate group chats and ensure community safety.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve app performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843).

3. Legal Basis for Processing (Ghana DPA)

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012, we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: When you explicitly agree to data collection (e.g., location sharing, health data input).
  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the Services you requested.
  • Legal Obligation: Compliance with health regulations and law enforcement requests.
  • Legitimate Interests: Fraud prevention, network security, and service improvement.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Supabase (cloud database), Firebase (push notifications), and analytics providers under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Healthcare Partners: Verified wellness centers may see anonymized demand data to improve services. No personally identifiable health data is shared without your explicit consent.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by Ghanaian courts, regulators, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data will remain protected under this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Health data at rest is encrypted with AES-256.
  • Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures users can only access their own records.
  • Regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments.
  • Staff access to user data is restricted and logged.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with Services. Health data is retained for 7 years after account deletion to comply with Ghanaian medical record regulations, after which it is securely destroyed. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

7. Your Rights Under Ghana Data Protection Act

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (“right to be forgotten”), subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object: Opt out of direct marketing and certain automated decision-making.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@4ourlife.health or use the in-app data request feature. We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will delete the information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is primarily stored on Supabase servers. Some service providers (e.g., Firebase, Sentry) may process data outside Ghana. All international transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions compliant with Ghana Data Protection Act requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website or use an app. They help the site recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferences and settings. “Similar technologies” include local storage, web beacons, and pixel tags.
