Store QR codes — print check sheet

Generated by scripts/generate-store-qr.js. Every PNG below was decoded again after generation and matched its URL exactly. Scan one with a phone camera before sending anything to print.

Before this goes to print

  • Do not crop the white margin. The 4-module quiet zone is part of the code.
  • Do not upscale a PNG. Place the SVG, or regenerate at 2048 for large format.
  • Distance rule of thumb: viewing distance ÷ 10 = minimum code width. Read from 2 m at a roll-up, the code must be at least 200 mm wide.
  • No logo overlay is applied. Adding one needs error correction H and an overlay under ~10% of the area.
  • Re-run the script if either store ID ever changes; it re-verifies against store/README.md and fails loudly on drift.
